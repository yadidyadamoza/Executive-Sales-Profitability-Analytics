# 📊 Executive Sales & Profitability Analytics

## 📌 Overview
An executive-level **Power BI** dashboard designed to track overall sales performance, analyze profitability margins, and uncover key revenue drivers across product categories and customer segments.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
* **BI Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** Excel / CSV (Sales & Financial Dataset)
* **Data Modeling & DAX:** Custom DAX measures for Revenue, Gross Profit Margin %, YOY/MOM Growth, and Sales Volume.

![Sales & Profitability Dashboard](dashboard_preview.png)

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Revenue & Profitability:** Monitored total sales revenue, net profit, and overall profit margins across all business units.
* **Product & Category Breakdown:** Evaluated sales volume and profitability across main product lines to identify top revenue drivers.
* **Sales Trends:** Analyzed historical performance and seasonal sales trends to support executive decision-making.
